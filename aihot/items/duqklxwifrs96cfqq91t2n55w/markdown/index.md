# Anthropic 因 AI agent 工时达人类 6 倍而大幅减少初级员工招聘

> 原标题：Anthropic has nearly stopped hiring junior staff because AI agents now work six times as many hours

- 来源：The Decoder
- 发布时间：2026-10-18T13:22:26.000Z
- MyHOT：https://reisen-ww.github.io/hot-pages/aihot/items/duqklxwifrs96cfqq91t2n55w
- 原文：https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-has-nearly-stopped-hiring-junior-staff-because-ai-agents-now-work-six-times-as-many-hours/

## 摘要

Anthropic 内部研究显示，技术部门初级岗位占比自 2025 年初从 21% 降至 14%，新招初级员工占比从 27% 降至 9%，原因是其 AI agent 工作时长已达人类员工的 6 倍。
