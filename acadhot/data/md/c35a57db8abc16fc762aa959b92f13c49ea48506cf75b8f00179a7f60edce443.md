> 原标题：Drastic improvement in MHC class I epitope prediction through training data augmentation, generative adversarial network modeling of selfness, and consideration of homology to class II epitopes.

## 摘要

Oh等团队在bioRxiv发表癌症疫苗新抗原预测框架DeepOmicsNeo，通过合成伪结合体与非结合体增强数据稀缺性（模块DeepOmicsNeo-Binding），利用6000万人类自我肽训练生成对抗网络（模块DeepOmicsNeo-Selfness）量化自我性，并基于类II表位同源性中心区建模（模块DeepOmicsNeo-H2）。
